Identify Downtrends:
- Uses 1h closed candles (BTC/USDT)
- previous candle is red (close < open)
- triggers on every candle following a red candle

Confirm Uptrend:
- Evaluate the 1h candle that triggered the downtrend signal
- Record the closing price of the signal candle as the "indicator" baseline
- The next candle must close above the indicator to confirm the uptrend AND if the low of the candle is below the indicator the close then the confirmed trend can't start until the next candle
- Once confirmed, the trend remains valid for subsequent 1h candles as long as their closing price stays above the indicator

Confirm Uptrend (Three Consecutive Green Candles):
- Requires three consecutive 1h green candles (close > open)
- Record the closing price of the 1st green candle as the "indicator" baseline
- Starting at the 3rd green candle, the uptrend is confirmed and remains valid as long as the low of subsequent candles stays strictly above the indicator
- The uptrend is invalidated if the low of any candle is at or below the indicator
