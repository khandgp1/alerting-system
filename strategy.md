Confirm Uptrend (Three Consecutive Green Candles):
- Requires three consecutive 1h green candles (close > open)
- Record the closing price of the 1st green candle as the "indicator" baseline
- Starting at the 3rd green candle, the uptrend is confirmed and remains valid as long as the low of subsequent candles stays strictly above the indicator
- The uptrend is invalidated if the low of any candle is at or below the indicator
- The 4th, 5th, or any further consecutive green candle in the same unbroken streak does NOT start a new signal
- If the consecutive green streak is broken by one or more non-green candles and then a new set of 3 consecutive green candles forms — even while the original trend's indicator is still valid — that new 3-candle pattern generates a new independent signal

Confirm Uptrend (Close Above Previous High):
- Possible Uptrend Signal: A green candle (close > open) closes strictly above the high of the candle immediately before it.
- Indicator: Marked at candle n-1 — at the close if candle n-1 is green (close > open), or at the open if candle n-1 is red (close < open).
- Uptrend Confirmation: The open of the candle immediately before the signal candle (n-1) is lower than the open of the candle three candles before the signal candle (n-3).
- Notation: Given candles [n-3, n-2, n-1, n], uptrend is confirmed if candle n is green, close[n] > high[n-1], and open[n-1] < open[n-3].
- The uptrend is confirmed at the signal candle (n). It is invalidated only if the low of a candle following the signal candle is at or below the indicator.
